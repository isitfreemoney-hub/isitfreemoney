Frequently asked questions

Is IsItfreemoney a public service?

No. It is a personal desktop tool used by its owner for the owner's own TikTok account.

Does it log in to my TikTok account?

It only signs in the owner, through TikTok's own Login Kit screen. The app never sees or stores a TikTok password.

What does it store?

A TikTok access token on the owner's computer, and a list of which videos were already posted. Nothing is stored on a server, because the app has none.

Can I remove the app's access to a TikTok account?

Yes. In TikTok go to Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, choose the app and remove it. Deleting the token file from the computer also removes it. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the app change my videos?

No. It uploads the video exactly as the owner chose it, and it adds no watermark, logo or promotional text.
