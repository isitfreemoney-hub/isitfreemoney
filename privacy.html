Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

IsItfreemoney ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used by one person to upload their own edited video clips to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the owner's TikTok open ID, plus the display name and username needed to show which account will be used. The app uses these only to post the owner's own videos.

What the app stores

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not send it, or any video, to any server other than TikTok. It collects no data about other TikTok users or viewers, and it has no analytics, advertising or cookies.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties.

Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and by deleting the token file from the computer.
