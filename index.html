Post your own video clips to your own TikTok account

IsItfreemoney is a desktop tool for a short-video creator. It connects to the creator's TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, shows a preview of an edited video from the creator's own computer, and posts it with the TikTok Content Posting API after the creator has chosen every option.

What the app does

Sign in with TikTok

The owner signs in once with TikTok Login Kit and approves the permissions. The app shows the account name it will post to, so there is never a doubt about which account is used.

Preview and caption

The owner picks one of their finished videos, watches a preview, and edits the caption (up to 2,200 characters) before anything is sent.

Choose every option

Who can view the video, comments, Duet, Stitch and commercial-content disclosure are all chosen by the owner for every post. Nothing is pre-selected.

Post or send as draft

The video is uploaded directly to TikTok. The owner can post it, or send it to their TikTok inbox as a draft and finish it in the TikTok app.

How a post works

1
Sign in. TikTok asks the owner to approve the app. The app receives an access token that stays on the owner's computer.
2
Pick a video. The app lists the owner's finished videos and plays a preview.
3
Set the options. The owner types the caption, chooses who can view the video, switches comments, Duet and Stitch on only if wanted, and states whether the video promotes a brand, product or service.
4
Confirm and post. The owner agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and the Branded Content Policy when it applies) and presses Post. The app then shows TikTok's processing status.

Who it is for

The app is built for one person: its owner, posting to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, it has no advertising, and it never posts anything the owner has not chosen and confirmed.

Your data

The app keeps the TikTok access token only on the owner's computer and sends videos only to TikTok. It collects nothing about other TikTok users and shares nothing with third parties. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
